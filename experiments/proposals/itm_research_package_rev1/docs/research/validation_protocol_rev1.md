# Measurement, inference, and replication protocol

**Review draft.** The frozen development data can show whether a change improves predictions on these campaigns and whether its proposed mechanism survives a controlled comparison. A claim that it improves ITM on new deployments also needs a frozen candidate and an independent campaign. All counts below come from the pinned repository metadata, input tables, and previously recorded outputs. All proposed tests await authorization.

**Quantity and sign.** The target is basic transmission loss in dB. For a free-space-referenced attenuation record, the builder uses $L_b=32.45+20\log_{10}(f_{\mathrm{MHz}})+20\log_{10}(d_{\mathrm{km}})+A$. The error is prediction minus observation. Positive error means excessive predicted loss. The Phase 2 50% prediction is compared with a spot measurement. The Virginia investigators typically used a median over about ten minutes. The long-term archive represents distributions of generally hourly medians with different recording periods. Its authors identify seasonal coverage, records as short as three weeks in their study, imperfect path information, and empirical climatology as contributors to differences. The repository's cleaned `length` field also contains short records such as `48 hr`. Use explicit units when parsing duration. A missing season code supplies no evidence of all-season coverage. [Hufford and Steele, §§2–3 and 6](https://its.ntia.gov/umbraco/surface/download/publication?reportNumber=91-282.pdf) · [Longley, Reasoner, and Fuller, introduction and pp. 13–14](https://its.ntia.gov/publications/download/OT-TRER-16.pdf) · [ITS data guide and archives](https://its.ntia.gov/data/legacy-radio-propagation-data)

**Censoring.** The original `phase_2.txt`, lines 14–23, describes a special flag for signals below sensitivity. The recorded attenuation lower bound already incorporates a 5 dB conservative adjustment. The repository drops missing sentinels, subtracts 500 from flagged entries, takes the median over valid repeat values, and marks the combined row censored if any repeat is flagged. Monotonicity of the median makes this an operational lower bound even with mixed repeats. It need not equal the unknown true loss. Keep the stored bound and the original gate. A secondary diagnostic can report the shortfall $\max(0,B_i-\hat L_i)$ in dB, the fraction of affected path IDs, and paired changes by site/band. A prediction above the bound establishes consistency with that observation. It does not establish the actual path loss. ## Existing decision rule

Run the official evaluator only in a later authorized implementation phase. Freeze the model specification, all cutoffs, the chosen parent, units, and primary directional contrast before observing candidate scores. `000-baseline` is the best kept model at this revision. `R02` is the feasible recalibration comparator. `502` is an informative rejected parent or control. Each comparison should name the parent whose incremental mechanism it tests and show the guardrail comparison to the current best. [Framework, acceptance rule](https://github.com/nicklasorte/ITM_ai_experiment/blob/5d39122275ef2b1b9c93be1e72007e61a90b53cc/FRAMEWORK.md#L417) · [Archive](https://github.com/nicklasorte/ITM_ai_experiment/blob/5d39122275ef2b1b9c93be1e72007e61a90b53cc/results/archive_rev1.txt)

1. Retain the physical rationale and prediction before the run. Compute the unrounded full and screened composite gains as current best minus candidate. Each must reach **0.3 dB**, and each paired 95% bootstrap interval must have lower endpoint above zero. The repository uses 500 draws and seed 1, resampling path IDs within each Phase 2 site and within each long-term fold. All rows associated with a sampled path travel together. The screen membership is fixed.
2. Each Phase 2 site's path-weighted RMS may increase by at most **0.5 dB**. Each new fitted parameter must earn at least **0.5 dB** on the primary score. Count fitted coefficients and any thresholds selected from measurements. Cite fixed physical constants and record numerical tolerances separately.
3. The censored violation rate is the **unweighted row fraction** of finite predictions below their stored bound. Equality passes. The maximum increase is **one percentage point** over the current best. Stock ITM violated 573/2,045 bounds, or 28.01956%. At this revision the largest passing count is 593/2,045. The printed 29.0% ceiling is rounded. Require finite output over every applicable candidate row. Reporting percent alone can conceal changed coverage.
4. Pass the implemented physics battery. Its largest step on each of five distance/frequency/height sweeps can exceed stock's maximum step by at most 1 dB. Reciprocity is the 95th percentile of forward/reverse absolute differences on 300 sampled uncensored rows, limited to the larger of 0.1 dB and stock's value + 0.1 dB. The real-row minimum excess loss must exceed or equal `min(−6.1, stock minimum) − 0.1` dB. The battery checks finite, real outputs. Run-specific analytic limits, gate-crossing sweeps, and maximum directional discrepancies supplement these checks. A median-only battery cannot validate a time-tail modification.

Each cell receives equal weight. Within cells the evaluator uses reciprocal path row count as a weight computed on the eligible row pool, then averages the cell RMS values. Long-term level averages these cell summaries across available 1, 10, 50, 90, and 99% levels. Shape uses the error in $L_p-L_{50}$ at 1, 10, 90, and 99%. The official composite gives equal weight to Phase 2 level, long-term level, and long-term shape. It is not pooled row RMSE. ## Proposed tests of the physical claim

**Open and concealed companions.** Match `O` and `C` on campaign, nominal path, frequency, both antenna heights and polarization. Exclude ambiguous multiple matches. Retain detection status. In VA further require identical profile hash, spacing, climate and refractivity. The frozen metadata have 465 exact pairs on 67 nominal paths, of which 342 pairs have two detected outcomes. A deterministic terrain-only model with the same inputs must give equal predictions in each pair. For two measured losses $a,b$ and a common prediction $m$,

$$\tfrac12[(m-a)^2+(m-b)^2]=[m-(a+b)/2]^2+(a-b)^2/4.$$

Thus the equal-weight within-pair RMS floor is $|a-b|/2$. It quantifies a limit of the representation for this pair. It is not a floor on the official composite. When only C is censored, $L_C-L_O\ge B_C-L_O$; when only O is censored, $L_C-L_O\le L_C-B_O$. Two lower bounds do not identify the contrast. Variability, measurement date, and local siting can all contribute. The historical report treats near companions as the same nominal path. Use `siting` only to adjudicate residuals. [Hufford and Steele, §§2, 3 and 6](https://its.ntia.gov/umbraco/surface/download/publication?reportNumber=91-282.pdf)

At R1, the same label/height/frequency match gives 597 O/C pairs on seven nominal paths, 591 with both detections. Each R1 path/siting ID has its own stored profile. Compare the change in predicted $L_C-L_O$ with its measured contrast. Record the change in geometry as a possible explanation. Apply no equal-input floor. R2 supplies 338 matched pairs on four nominal paths (232 both detected), which permits only a limited sensitivity. Use one summary per nominal path before aggregating. R3 has no censored observations and no concealed sitings. A zero numerator on a zero denominator supplies no gate evidence. [McQuate, Harman, and Barsis, §2, pp. 5–6](https://its.ntia.gov/umbraco/surface/download/publication?reportNumber=essa_erl_65-ITS_58.pdf) · [Phase 2 links](https://github.com/nicklasorte/ITM_ai_experiment/blob/5d39122275ef2b1b9c93be1e72007e61a90b53cc/data/links/phase2_links_rev1.csv)

**Ridge count, range, and sensitivity.** R2's D03 detected and censored configurations have different distance and frequency distributions. Its edge count is a recursive, depth-limited Deygout diagnostic. For fixed heights and geometry its normalized knife-edge parameter scales by the same positive frequency factor at every candidate point, preserving the maximizing point and its sign. A frequency effect within fixed geometry cannot be attributed to a changing D03 count. Use matched configurations and report frequency, heights, detection limit, distance, geometric class, candidate loss response, detected residual, and censored shortfall. Predeclare the primary mechanism contrast and a control class where its predicted correction is zero. Reject a ridge mechanism that only tracks detection sensitivity or whose result disappears when paths are weighted equally. [D03 script and grouping](https://github.com/nicklasorte/ITM_ai_experiment/blob/5d39122275ef2b1b9c93be1e72007e61a90b53cc/experiments/D03-r2-dead-paths/r2_dead_paths_rev1.py#L29) · [D03 summary](https://github.com/nicklasorte/ITM_ai_experiment/blob/5d39122275ef2b1b9c93be1e72007e61a90b53cc/experiments/D03-r2-dead-paths/summary.txt)

**Uncertainty and independence.** Keep Phase 2 folds by campaign and all heights/frequencies on each path together. Long-term receiver coordinates rounded to 0.1° define groups assigned to five balanced folds. The official bootstrap resamples individual path IDs *within* those strata. For a sensitivity, resample complete nominal-path companions together in Phase 2, and complete receiver groups within fold for long-term. Show per-site and leave-one-site-out changes, numbers of contributing clusters and cells, and both endpoints of each interval. Keep one draw and identical masks for candidate and comparator. Phase 2 supplies four campaign groups, which limits geographic inference. If fitting is proposed, predefine a training-only inner grouping for parameter choice and evaluate on the untouched outer fold. Fixed-input structural models still face selection bias because investigators saw detailed results across earlier batches. Label all five proposed tests exploratory on these development data. Select at most one candidate before opening independent evidence, and retain failures. [Split construction](https://github.com/nicklasorte/ITM_ai_experiment/blob/5d39122275ef2b1b9c93be1e72007e61a90b53cc/data/build_links_rev1.m#L237) · [Dwork et al., 2015](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html)

**Replication decision.** Before terminal testing, freeze the candidate's implementation hash, acceptance rule, correction sign versus its ablation, applicability envelope, and primary outcome. An independent custodian should verify provenance and deduplicate paths, campaigns and observers from development. For terrain effects use a campaign with calibrated basic loss, profiles at an adequate resolution, AGL heights and a documented noise floor. For time-shape claims require repeated hourly medians over documented durations and seasons, with co-temporal comparison where available. Hold out complete route/terminal or campaign groups; adjacent drive samples and repeated hours provide limited independent replication. Seek the prespecified effect and lower paired cluster interval above zero on the predeclared primary metric, without a reversal of its negative control or unacceptable censored deterioration. If the data lack the targeted geometry, matched configurations, usable detection thresholds or adequate recording duration, report the claim as untested on that campaign. Ask the human custodian to conduct any approved sealed evaluation once after freezing the candidate. Do not use its result to retune and claim confirmation on the same sample.

For an external challenge, Hicks, McLean, and Ewan's 3.475 GHz, 185 km link has synchronous measurements with two receiver antennas. It exposes sensitivity to antenna beamwidth on the same path. Its two-day window and one link cannot establish a climatological tail or geographic generalization. Longer recordings on independent links and documented gain patterns would support that claim. Published summary curves can check plausibility. Raw row-level availability and independence need verification before treating them as a replication set. [Hicks et al., 2024, Table 1 and §§II–IV](https://its.ntia.gov/publications/download/Hicks_Tropo_URSI2024.pdf)
