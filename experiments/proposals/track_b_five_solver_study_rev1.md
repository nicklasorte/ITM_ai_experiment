## TB-W wide-angle PE
Hypothesis: wide-angle dispersion and impedance boundary improve conditional terrain loss versus narrow-angle PE under equal inputs. Scalar Helmholtz with envelope E=e^(ik0x)u: u_xx+2ik0u_x+u_zz+k0^2(n^2-1)u=0. For range-invariant operator Q=n^2+k0^-2 dzz, outgoing forward derivative u_x=ik0(sqrt(Q)-1)u; branch Im sqrt>=0 for evanescent modes, noncommuting range-varying operators not exact. Start homogeneous angular spectrum then Pade approximation, boundary and terrain. Freeze angle support from phase error over electrical length, not arbitrary 30 degrees. Inputs n(x,z), terrain, impedance, polarization, launch/extraction. C01/C02/C03/C05/C10. Reject on unresolved backward field, unstable evanescent branch, boundary reflection or grid error. Reference independent angular spectrum and TB-I. Literature Kuttler/Dockery 1991 abstract doi:10.1029/91RS00109; wide-angle terrain paper doi:10.1029/2000RS002560 (abstract/introduction). Full equation source audit pending.

## TB-G GO/UTD
Hypothesis: direct/reflected/diffracted coherent fields produce fast conditional loss on well-resolved high-frequency geometry. H=sum_j A_j exp(i k0 integral n ds) with complex reflection/diffraction factors and ray-tube spreading within A_j; no extra FSPL if A_j already absolute. Eikonal |grad S|^2=n^2. UTD wedge coefficient and transition functions must be fixed by polarization, wedge convention and incident/reflected ray geometry before code; no generic scalar knifeedge substitute. Hypothesis: explicit transverse geometry resolves systematic errors of 2D profile models. Scalar paraxial envelope 2ik0 u_x+u_yy+u_zz+k0^2(n^2-1)u=0. Start free-space transverse spectral solution, then finite-width PEC ridge, then impedance and atmosphere, with wide-angle upgrade only after convergence. C01/C03/C05/C06/C10.
