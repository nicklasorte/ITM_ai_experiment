## Decisions (B23 D23b)

1. Attribution: 870 rows have |g| >= 1 dB and 644 have |m| >= 1 dB (of 1400); each such row is an explicit counterexample to an additive-only reading of the C/O difference. Counterexamples exist: the twin difference is height substitution plus clutter term plus a model-baseline mismatch, not the P.2108-1 term alone.
2. Predictive evidence, strict: **INCONCLUSIVE** (rule not met). Every above-3 GHz figure is hold3, an extrapolation policy, not source-validated coverage.
2. Predictive evidence, exploratory (strict + limited): **INCONCLUSIVE** (rule not met). Every above-3 GHz figure is hold3, an extrapolation policy, not source-validated coverage.
3. Censored evidence is listed above and is reported separately; where detected-only support comes with worse informative-bound consistency, treat the result as mixed.

Measurement caveat: Part I of ERL 65-ITS 58 estimates an unlogged transmitter impedance change of up to about 2 dB between open and concealed siting; differential calibration uncertainty for C/O contrasts is therefore at least that large for R1 and unquantified elsewhere, so the 1 dB resolution cannot separate small effects.
