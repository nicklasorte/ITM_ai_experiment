# Batch B11: NLCD land cover at open Phase 2 terminals (H20/H21)

Planner: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-22. Parent: `best` (707). Score with
`Best="707-cascaded-isolated-clutter-at-r"`. Runs only after PR #6 (land
cover file and `cfg.row.path_id`) is merged and pulled on the Mac.

## Purpose

D04 found VA's open, terrain-clear paths 17-25 dB lossier than predicted,
with the gap rising with frequency: consistent with unmodelled clutter along
the VA routes (research plan H21; package H20). Nick approved a public
land-cover input on 2026-11-22. This batch tests whether ITU-R P.2108-1
section 3.1, applied at open mobile terminals by their NLCD class, explains
that bias without hurting the other sites.

## Input and mapping (fixed before extraction)

`data/landcover/phase2_terminals_nlcd_rev1.csv` (PR #6): NLCD 2001 class at
each terminal pixel, forest and developed fractions within 100 m, and the
Annual NLCD 1985-2023 change count. Mapping in
`itm_variant/landcover_lookup_rev1.m`: 41, 42, 43, 90 to trees/forest; 22
suburban; 23 urban; 24 dense urban; else none. Concealed rows keep 707's
mapping. Path loss at max(h, R), A_h held at 3 GHz above 3 GHz (as 707).

Caveats recorded before any score: NLCD 2001 is about 40 years after the
measurements; 143 of 515 mobile-terminal pixels changed cover at least once
since 1985; Phase 2 coordinates are given to 4 decimal places at best.

## Experiments

### 711-nlcd-open-clutter
- Change (launcher overrides): 707's five settings plus
  `"terminal_clutter_open":"nlcd"`.
- Source: ITU-R P.2108-1 section 3.1 and Table 3; NLCD 2001 (USGS/MRLC).
- Predicted: R3 mean error rises by 5-7 dB and R3 RMS by more than 0.5 dB
  (fails the site gate); R1 RMS rises; VA RMS changes by less than 1 dB;
  composite worse than 707. Censored violations fall (more loss
  everywhere). Most likely: rejected. Falsifies H20/H21 as specified (pixel
  class with P.2108 section 3.1) if R3 and R1 worsen while VA barely moves.
- Confidence: medium-high.

### 712-nlcd-open-forest-only
- Rationale registered before 711 ran: vegetation alone (package H20) at
  open terminals, no developed classes, because R3's developed pixels drive
  most of 711's predicted harm.
- Change (launcher overrides): 707's five settings plus
  `"terminal_clutter_open":"nlcd_forest"` (added after 711; 37 tests pass).
- Smoke (model only), change on open and A rows against 707: R1 11.5 % of
  rows affected, +2.1 dB on all (+18.3 on affected); R2 39.3 %, +6.4 (+16.3);
  R3 2.0 %, +0.3 (+16.1); VA 19.5 %, +1.7 (+8.8).
- Predicted (written after 711's result and this smoke test, before 712
  runs; scaled from 711's response): R3 RMS within 0.5 dB of 707 (6.8);
  R1 RMS 11.8-12.8 and R2 RMS 19.0-19.6, both more than 0.5 dB worse than
  707 (site gate fails); VA RMS within 0.5 dB of 12.9; Phase 2 cell
  14.4-15.2; composite 12.8-13.1, worse than 707; censored 18-21 %. Most
  likely: rejected, which would leave no form of the NLCD point-class test
  that helps VA without hurting R1 or R2.
- Confidence: medium.
