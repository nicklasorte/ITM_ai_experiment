## 0. Assumptions checked against the code before registering

B37 registered a wrong assumption about where a switch acts. Each
assumption below was checked in the code on 2026-11-27:

- The delta construction replaces ITM's reference attenuation on every
  row, line of sight included (`itm_p2p_core_exp.m` lines 105–123).
- The cascade (`multi_edge_diffraction_rev1`, method
  `p526_cascaded_isolated`, lines 216–238) is L = J_p + T (L_t + L_r + C)
  when the principal edge has ν > −0.78, else 0. J_p is the principal
  knife edge, L_t and L_r are the isolation-weighted secondary edges, T =
  1 − exp(−J_p/6) (ITU-R P.526-11 eq. (52)) and C = 10 + 0.04 D (km)
  (eq. (51)). The same method gives both L_ba (actual profile) and L_bs
  (zero-height profile).
- Bullington (`"bullington"`) includes ITU-R P.526-16's eq. (57)
  correction; `"bullington_uc"` drops it (existing switch,
  `delta_bullington_uc`).
- Profile sample i of the profile CSV is point i of `x` in the delta
  construction (`x = (0:np)' * xi`, line 874), and the `D.edges` indices
  the cascade returns index that same `x`.
- The NLCD runs in `data/landcover/phase2_profiles_nlcd_rev1.csv` cover
  samples 1 to n_points of all 515 Phase 2 profiles (checked by script).
- Earlier land-cover work, so this batch doesn't repeat it: open-terminal
  clutter by NLCD class (711, 712: rejected), P.833 woodland along the
  terminal rays (B17: screened as certain harm), 15 m canopy on every
  woodland profile sample (1806: certain harm), LANDFIRE canopy height near
  the terminals (B30: one site resolved, RMS gain 0.26 dB). None of these
  asked where on the path the diffraction happens or what covers the first
  Fresnel zone.

## 2. D38a: attribution inside the cascade step (model only)

**Population:** B36's rows (siting O, uncensored, 1 GHz or less, steepest
interval 10° or less): R1 1,427 rows on 29 paths decides. VA's 243 rows
are descriptive. The driver checks the counts.

**Arms** (all with `diffraction_bound = "none"`; every other switch at
stock; each row at 50 %, the evaluator's settings, the profile of
record):

| Arm | `diffraction` | Cascade term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | `delta_bullington` | (B37's C1) |
| C1u | `delta_bullington_uc` | Bullington without eq. (57) |
| P | `delta_cascaded_isolated_principal` (new) | L = J_p |
| U | `delta_cascaded_isolated_uc` (new) | L = J_p + T (L_t + L_r) |
| C2 | `delta_cascaded_isolated` | L = J_p + T (L_t + L_r + C) (B37's C2) |

The two new methods are report-only variants, off by default. They are
written in `multi_edge_diffraction_rev1` with their own tests before any
row runs (section 4).

Checks: C1 and C2 must equal B37's `L_C1` and `L_C2`
(`experiments/D37-pe-repair-and-707-parts/parts_rev1.csv`) to 1e-9 dB;
otherwise stop. P ≤ U ≤ C2 on L_ba for every row is a property of the
formula (T ≥ 0, L_t, L_r ≥ 0, C > 0), checked on L_ba directly. It doesn't
hold for the delta losses, because L_bs moves too.

**Steps:** Δ2 = (P − C1) + (U − P) + (C2 − U): principal against
Bullington, secondary edges, correction. The sum is an identity, checked,
not evidence.

**Statistics:** as B37 section 3: ratio-of-sums mean over rows, 95 %
interval over paths, 2,000 draws, seed 20261138, numpy default_rng, paths
sorted by `path_id`, draws of shape (2000, 29). Also by ITM mode (from B37's
`itm_mode`) and frequency, plain means. Descriptive: C1 − C1u
(Bullington's own correction).

**Reading rule:** a part carries Δ2 if its mean is at least half of mean Δ2
with the same sign and its interval excludes zero; otherwise "spread".

## 3. D38b: land cover at the edges and in the first Fresnel zone

**Population:** every Phase 2 row with siting O and not censored, at
every frequency, at the four sites (R1, R2, R3, VA), with a 707
prediction in `experiments/707r-rescore-screen36/predictions_phase2.csv`.
The driver reports the count; there's no registered count. Censored rows
are left out, as in B36.

**Inputs (all frozen):** the profile of record; `htx_m`, `hrx_m`, `f_mhz`
and `N_0` from the link table; NLCD 2001 by sample from
`data/landcover/phase2_profiles_nlcd_rev1.csv` (hash checked). Forest is
NLCD 41, 42, 43 and 90 (B11's mapping). The terrain is the profile of
record. NLCD gives a class, not a canopy height.

**Fresnel-zone footprint (per row):** the straight ray between the
antenna tips over the effective earth, with ITM's γ_e (B36's `itm_gamma`
rule). Interior sample i is in the footprint if ray(x_i) − (z_i + b_i) <
r_1(x_i), where b_i = x_i (d − x_i) γ_e / 2 (earth bulge) and r_1 =
sqrt(λ x_i (d − x_i) / d) (ITU-R P.526-16 eq. (2)). Blocked samples count
(the clearance is negative). f_F is the forest share of footprint samples.
A row with no footprint sample has f_F undefined and is counted and left
out. "Obstructed" means some interior sample is above the ray.

**Edge land cover (per row, descriptive):** the NLCD class at the C2
arm's principal edge (`edges_ba` from D38a's report-only field; the edge
with weight 1), on rows where the principal edge has ν > −0.78.

**Statistic:** within each site, y = e_707 = `var_lb_50` − `meas_lb_db`
and x = f_F are demeaned within strata of frequency × obstructed. The
slope is b = Σxy / Σx² (dB per unit forest share), with a 95 % interval
over paths (2,000 draws, seed 20261138, sites in the order R1, R2, R3,
VA, paths sorted, demeaning redone in every draw). The same for stock
(`itm_lb_50`), descriptive.

**Reading rule:** b is resolved at a site if its interval excludes zero
and |b| ≥ 5 dB (1 dB per 20 percentage points of forest; a planner
choice). Outcome **F−** if b is resolved negative at two or more sites and
positive at none (more forest in the zone, more loss than 707 predicts);
**F+** the reverse; **none** otherwise.

This is an association within strata, not a cause. Forest share goes with
terrain, distance and site, and the strata take out only frequency and
obstruction.

### D38a: inside the cascade step (model only)

R1 stratum (1,427 rows on 29 paths, one receiver site); 95 % interval over
paths, seed 20261138:

| Part | Mean (dB) | 95 % interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Δ2 = C2 − C1 (B37's cascade step) | +2.18 | +1.08 to +3.55 |
| Principal edge against Bullington (P − C1) | −4.04 | −5.11 to −3.00 |
| Secondary edges (U − P) | +2.25 | +1.08 to +3.66 |
| Correction C (C2 − U) | +3.97 | +2.91 to +5.00 |
| Descriptive: Bullington's own correction (C1 − C1u) | +3.84 | +2.80 to +4.85 |

**Reading (registered rule):** two parts each meet the rule: the
secondary edges and the correction. The principal edge goes the other way.
The rule assumed at most one part would meet it, so it has no label for
two; this records both.

**Descriptive regrouping (arithmetic on the same rows, not a registered
statistic):** both C1 and C2 carry a correction term, and the two are
almost equal (+3.84 and +3.97 dB). Measured against Bullington without its
correction (C1u):

- The principal knife edge alone: P − C1u = −0.20 dB.
- Adding the secondary edges: +2.25 dB.
- The difference between the two corrections: +0.13 dB.

So, read this way, the secondary edges account for almost all of the
cascade step on R1. The principal edge alone is close to uncorrected
Bullington, and the two corrections nearly cancel.

By ITM mode, R1 (plain means): on the 206 diffraction rows Δ2 is +9.46 dB,
with secondary edges +9.68. On the 1,221 line-of-sight rows Δ2 is
+0.95 dB, with secondary edges +1.00. By frequency, secondary edges add
+2.08 to +2.48 dB.

VA (243 rows on 61 paths; descriptive): same pattern, smaller. Δ2 is
+1.59, P − C1 is −2.60, secondary edges +1.59, correction +2.60, and
Bullington's correction +2.55.

### D38b: land cover in the first Fresnel zone

Population: 12,570 siting-O uncensored rows. 3,069 have no interior sample
inside the first Fresnel zone (2,198 of them at R3), so f_F is undefined
there and they're left out, which leaves 9,501 rows. The terrain is USGS
3DEP bare earth (FRAMEWORK.md), so canopy isn't in the profile heights.

Slope of 707's error on forest share, within frequency × obstructed strata
(dB per unit share; 95 % interval over paths):

| Site | Rows (paths) | Mean f_F | Rows with f_F ≥ 0.5 | b (707) | 95 % interval | Resolved | b (stock), descriptive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | 3,099 (47) | 0.11 | 388 | +7.6 | −1.4 to +18.1 | No | +26.6 (+17.2 to +37.1) |
| R2 | 2,142 (38) | 0.87 | 2,012 | −22.3 | −57.4 to −0.3 | Yes | −47.2 (−89.2 to −23.4) |
| R3 | 2,951 (53) | 0.006 | 0 | −105.2 | −198.9 to −26.6 | Yes | −115.4 (−244.4 to −7.4) |
| VA | 1,309 (257) | 0.52 | 944 | −4.7 | −10.1 to +1.2 | No | −8.7 (−14.7 to −2.5) |

**Outcome F− (registered rule):** at R2 and R3, rows whose first Fresnel
zone holds more forest have more loss than 707 predicts. No site is
resolved positive. **The outcome depends on R3:** without it only R2 is
resolved and the outcome would be "none". R2 also only just meets the rule
(its interval ends at −0.28).

Limits on that reading:

- **R3's slope is an extrapolation.** R3's forest share is at most 0.081,
  is above zero on 1,184 of 2,951 rows, and has a mean of 0.006. The −105 dB per unit share comes from rows
  with a few percent forest: about −2 dB at 2 % forest. Its interval
  excludes zero, but the rule's 5 dB size was meant for shares that vary
  over 0 to 1.
- **R2's forest share hardly varies:** it's 0.5 or more on 94 % of rows.
- **It's an association within frequency × obstruction strata, not a
  cause.** Forest share goes with terrain, distance and path.
- **Leaving out the 3,069 clear rows** selects the more obstructed paths,
  most of all at R3. Those rows aren't like the rest: their mean e_707 is
  −11.7 dB against −1.0 at R2, and −14.0 against −2.5 at VA.

Principal-edge land cover (obstructed rows where the C2 cascade has a
principal edge with ν > −0.78; descriptive):

| Site | Rows | Edge in forest | 707 mean error: edge forest / not forest | Stock: edge forest / not forest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | 2,039 | 16 % | +10.7 / +5.4 dB | +22.0 / +4.2 dB |
| R2 | 1,973 | 84 % | +0.4 / +3.6 dB | +17.1 / +24.0 dB |
| R3 | 1,159 | 0 % | none / +6.6 dB | none / +9.1 dB |
| VA | 799 | 36 % | −0.2 / −0.5 dB | −1.9 / −2.9 dB |

At R1, both models over-predict loss most where the principal edge is
forest, by +10.7 dB for 707 and +22.0 dB for stock. That's the wrong way
for trees to explain it. Inferred, not tested: R1's forested edges are
foothill ridges, where the terrain, not the canopy, sets the loss.

### What this means for improving ITM (planner's reading, not part of a rule)

- **On R1, the secondary edges are the lead.** The cascade's secondary
  edges add about 2.2 dB on R1's gentle rows and about 9.7 dB on its
  diffraction-mode rows. There, 707 already predicts too much loss (B36).
  1801 (one secondary edge, B18) removed a principal-edge jump but added
  censored violations. A repair would have to keep secondary edges where
  they help (VA's signed error, B36) and limit them where they over-count.
- **Forest in the Fresnel zone goes with missing loss** at R2, the site
  where 707's error is largest (RMS 18.4 dB on these rows), and at R3,
  where it's smallest (6.6 dB); the R3 result rests on small forest shares
  and the outcome needs it. Both sites are outside the PE's domain. That
  points at a vegetation term along the path, weighted by the Fresnel
  zone, not at the terminals. Earlier forms (1806: canopy as terrain on
  every woodland sample; B17: P.833 along the terminal rays) were screened
  as certain harm. A Fresnel-weighted form hasn't been tried. It would
  need canopy height (the held data PR D2) and its own registration.

### Departures from the registration

- Section 3 defines the principal edge as "the edge with weight 1". A
  secondary edge can also have weight 1 (2,913 three-edge rows have weight
  1 on the transmitter-side edge), so the run used the middle of
  [t p r], the cascade's own order. The same rule is in the fixed test and
  in `d38a_parts_rev1.m`.
- The edge table is limited to obstructed rows, which wasn't registered.
- `multiedge_ref_rev1.mat` was saved on the Mac before the code edit but
  committed with it (616d42e), so git doesn't show the order.
