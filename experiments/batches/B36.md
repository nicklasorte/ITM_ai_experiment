# Batch B36: a parabolic-equation physics reference on gentle Phase 2 paths, against 707 (D36, diagnostic, no score)

Planner: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-26. Nick: "Keep working on improving
ITM". Follows the greenfield proposal's E03 and the planner's
recommendation the same day: run a converged physics solver on the same
frozen inputs, to learn whether 707's remaining error on these paths comes
from ITM's propagation approximations or from the inputs.

**Status: registered 2026-11-26, not run. No PE result has been compared
with a measurement.**

D-series: nothing here is scored, counts toward k, or changes `best`. The
Phase 2 data are development data, inspected many times; this is a
diagnostic of where error comes from, not a validation.

## 1. Question

On Phase 2 paths where the terrain is gentle enough for the solver to be
valid, does a 2D parabolic-equation (PE) solution on the same profiles
predict the 50 % basic transmission loss better than 707?

- If it does, ITM's (and 707's) propagation approximations are a material
  error source there, and the PE field can show which ITM term fails.
- If it doesn't, the remaining error on these paths comes from what
  neither model has (clutter near the terminals, terrain detail finer than
  30 m, ground, measurement), and improving ITM further there needs new
  inputs or data, not better propagation physics.

## 2. The reference

`experiments/batches/pe2d_rev1.m`, validated by `pe2d_tests_rev1.m`
(commit 8c4cf0e; all six tests pass):

| Test | Reference | Error |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 flat conducting ground | 2D two-ray | 0.003 dB |
| T2 knife edge over flat ground | four-path Kirchhoff (Fresnel integral) | 0.09 dB |
| T3 smooth earth beyond the horizon, 230 and 910 MHz | ITU-R P.452-18 §4.2.2 (`spherical_earth_diffraction_rev1`) | −0.26, −0.40 dB |
| T6 uniform 5.7° slope | two-ray of the tilted plane | 0.16 dB |
| T4, T5 absorber and grid halving, R1 path 52.6 km, 910 MHz | self | < 0.001 dB |

Formulation (Ryan, NOSC TR 1453, 1991): narrow-angle split-step Fourier
PE; earth curvature and standard refraction through m − 1 = γ_e z with
ITM's own γ_e; perfectly conducting ground (horizontal polarization, which
every Phase 2 row uses); terrain-following coordinates (exact ground at
z' = 0, slope changes as phase factors); free-space normalization by a
run of the same source on the same grid, along the direct ray.

**Validity limits found while building it** (recorded as results of the
build, not of B36):

- The staircase terrain method absorbs instead of reflecting where the
  terrain is above the grid bottom, and its loss grows as the range step
  shrinks (2.5 to 3 dB per halving on R2 path R2-010-T2). It isn't used.
- The terrain-following method is valid for small slopes only. On the same
  R2 path (steepest 30 m interval 41°) it converges to values about 20 dB
  above the staircase's; neither is credible there.
- So the reference covers only gentle paths: R1 and Virginia, and one R2
  path. R2 and R3, where 707's errors are largest, are outside its domain.
  B36 can't say anything about them.

## 3. Population and inputs

- **Rows:** `data/links/phase2_links_rev1.csv`, siting O (open; 707's
  terminal term doesn't act, and the PE has no clutter either), not
  censored, f ≤ 1000 MHz (230, 410, 751, 910 MHz), on paths whose steepest
  profile interval in `data/profiles/phase2/` is 10° or less. Count before
  the run: R1 1,427 rows on 29 paths; VA 243 rows on 61 paths; R2 13 rows
  on 1 path. The driver recomputes these and stops if they differ.
- **Per row:** profile as the evaluator reads it (`load_profiles_rev1`,
  the CSV terrain of record); transmitter height `htx_m`, receiver height
  `hrx_m`, frequency, from the link table; γ_e from the row's N_0 exactly
  as ITM computes it (`initialize_point_to_point`: N_s = N_0
  exp(−h_sys/9460) with h_sys the mean elevation of the middle 80 % of the
  profile; γ_e = 157e-9 (1 − 0.04665 exp(N_s/179.3))).
- **Solver settings (planner-chosen):** `pe2d_rev1` defaults:
  terrain-following, narrow-angle, λ/4 vertical step, range step at most
  min(spacing, 30 m), margin max(1000 m, 3√(λd), 0.25 γ_e d²), absorber
  half the domain at 2e-2 Np/m, source pattern 3 dB down at 20°, receiver
  power averaged over the last 20 wavelengths of range.
- **Prediction:** L_PE = fs_db + A_PE, with fs_db from the link table (the
  free-space loss the measured values use). The PE predicts a median under
  standard refraction; it has no time variability.
- **Other arms:** 707 (`var_lb_50`) and stock ITM (`itm_lb_50`) from
  `experiments/707r-rescore-screen36/predictions_phase2.csv`, joined by
  `link_id`.
- **Per-path convergence screen (registered):** for each path, at its
  highest frequency in the population and its first receiver height, the
  PE is rerun with λ/8 and half the range step. If the result moves by more
  than 1 dB, every row of that path is excluded and counted.

## 4. Registered rule

Error e = L_model − meas_lb_db. Strata: R1 and VA (deciding); R2
(descriptive, one path). In each deciding stratum: D = mean |e_PE| −
mean |e_707| over the rows; 95 % percentile interval from 2,000 bootstrap
draws over paths (`numpy.random.default_rng(20261136)`, R1 first, then VA;
paths sorted by `path_id`; each draw `rng.integers(0, n, size=(2000, n))`
with n the stratum's path count). "Resolved": |D| ≥ 1 dB and the interval
excludes zero.

- **Outcome P (physics better):** D resolved and negative in both R1 and
  VA.
- **Outcome M (707 better):** D resolved and positive in both.
- **Outcome N:** anything else (no clear difference or mixed). N isn't
  equivalence.

If convergence exclusions remove more than 20 % of a stratum's rows, that
stratum is descriptive and the outcome can't be P or M.

Also reported, not deciding: the same D against stock ITM; mean signed
error per arm by stratum and by frequency; D by frequency; the point value
(no range averaging) in place of the averaged one; the share of rows where
the PE and 707 differ by less than 1 dB.

Planner-chosen values: the 10° slope limit, the 1 dB size, the 1 dB
convergence screen, the 20 % rule, the solver settings, seed 20261136.

## 5. Predictions (planner, before the run)

- Outcome N 55 %, M 25 %, P 20 %. Reason: on gentle open paths 707 is
  close to ITM, ITM's line-of-sight and smooth-earth terms are already
  reasonable, and the errors there likely come from trees and buildings
  near the terminals (VA is wooded), which the PE lacks as much as 707
  does.
- Mean signed error of the PE is negative (predicts too little loss) in
  both strata: 70 %.
