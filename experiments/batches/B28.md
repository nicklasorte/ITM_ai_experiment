## 1. Why now

Nick, 2026-11-25: "Ignore the lockbox all together. Just keep going." Phase
plan rev 5 stopped the structural search on the frozen inputs and left one
untried route: calibrating the constants of the kept structure (roadmap
phase 3). Rev 5 had gated it on the lockbox; that gate is removed. The
censored gate stays as written in FRAMEWORK.md's acceptance rule (no
decision was made to change it).

Physical rationale (one sentence per group, logged before the run):

- **2801, diffraction weighting:** ITM's smooth-earth weight constant (25.1),
  and its knife-edge and clutter-term scales, were fitted to 1960s data with
  ITM's own double knife edge; 707 replaces that knife edge with a
  profile-based cascade, so the weights fitted for the old term may not suit
  the new one.
- **2802, line-of-sight blend:** the two-ray versus extrapolated-diffraction
  weight scales with `f Δh / d`; 707's obstructed line-of-sight rows now take
  the profile-based loss, which changes what the blend joins.
- **2803, time spread:** the two sides of ITM's time-variability spread set
  the long-term shape and level away from 50 %; R02 moved only the lower side
  on stock ITM (0.8), and 707 changes the median those spreads sit on.

uses training folds only; held-out predictions are scored with `score_rev1`.
Search ranges are the harness's own. MaxEvals 40, Seed 1.

doesn't return 707 with ITM's constants.

## 3. Acceptance (from FRAMEWORK.md and program.md; nothing new)

Bonferroni count: 2801 is k = 7, 2802 is k = 8, 2803 is k = 9. The decision
uses the paired 99.5 % lower bound (`lo995`, 2,000 draws), which is at least
as strict as 1 − 0.05/k for k = 7 to 9.

A run is kept only if all hold:

1. Screened gain `lo995` > 0, and screened point gain ≥ 0.3 dB.
2. Complexity cost: screened point gain ≥ 0.5 dB per new free parameter
   (2801 ≥ 1.5, 2802 ≥ 0.5, 2803 ≥ 1.0).
3. Full composite no worse than 707 by more than 0.3 dB.
4. No site RMS worse than 707 by more than 0.5 dB.
5. Censored violations at most 1 point above 707 (22.8 % → ≤ 23.8 %).
6. Physics battery PASS at the median fitted constants.
7. Fitted constants inside the harness's plausible ranges, and not at a range
   bound in a majority of folds (a bound hit means the range, not the data,
   set the value; report it).

The harness's own censored constraint is against stock ITM + 1 point on the
training folds; check 5 against 707 is applied afterward.

Runs 2801, 2802 and 2803 (MATLAB batch jobs 260 to 262) all stopped in
fold 1 with `itmexp:infeasible`: "no feasible trial, not even ITM's own
constants." No output folders were written and nothing was scored.

Cause (harness defect, not a model result): commit 2f0692b (2026-11-23,
report-only) added `checks.continuity_pointwise` to the physics battery.
`cv_calibrate_rev1`'s constraint loop treats every field whose name starts
with `continuity_` as a step check and reads `variant_max_step_db`, which
that field doesn't have. The read throws outside the trial's `try`, so every
trial fails. R02 ran on 2026-11-21, before that commit. With report-only
fields skipped, 707 at ITM's constants has a physics margin of −0.10 dB
(feasible); every non-report check's margin was computed from the same
battery output.

PR #14 (merged, afe4b23) fixed that. The reruns (jobs 263 to 265) then
stopped in fold 1 for a second reason: `predict` didn't pass the row
metadata `cfg.row` that 707's terminal clutter needs. PR #15 (merged,
fb78249) fixed that.

### Decision

No held-out predictions were written or scored (folds 1 to 3 were
predicted in memory and lost when fold 4 stopped), so checks 1 to 7 weren't applied and
nothing is scored; k stays 6. B28 closes. Rerunning would need the
harness's censored constraint to reference the parent (707's own training
rate + 1 point, as the acceptance rule in FRAMEWORK.md gates it) instead of
stock ITM. That is a rule change to frozen `eval/`, so it's Nick's call, as
PR #14 and PR #15 were (Nick approved both in chat on 2026-11-25). On the fold 1 to 3
training evidence above, the planner expects no keep even with that change.
