## Why there is no candidate

Phase plan rev 4 allowed one R1 candidate if the public sources offer a
smooth-surface construction that differs from 1803 and 1804 in mechanism. It
left one source check open: whether ITU-R P.1812-8 §5.6 applies the
obstruction adjustment. I checked it, and P.2001 too.

| Source (public) | Smooth-surface construction for diffraction | Same as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ITU-R P.452-18 §5.1.6.2–5.1.6.3, eq. (147)–(153d) | Least-squares line, then lowered by the obstruction term h_obs·g, clamped to ground | 707 |
| ITU-R P.452-18 §5.1.6.4, eq. (154a)–(156) | Surface independent of the antennas, no obstruction adjustment | 1803 (out: new 30.7 dB height failures on R2-080-T4) |
| ITU-R P.1812-8 Attachment 1 §5.6.1–5.6.2, eq. (83)–(89d) | Fitted line, then eq. (88c)–(88f) lower each end by h_obs·g_t and h_obs·g_r when h_obs > 0, clamped by eq. (89a)–(89d) | 707 |
| ITU-R P.2001-5 §3.8, eq. (27)–(37b) | Fitted line, then eq. (35d)–(35f) obstruction adjustment, clamped by eq. (36a–b); the only surface without the adjustment (eq. (30)–(32)) is for ducting and layer reflection | 707 |
| ITM effective heights (NTIA TR-82-100) | Heights from terrain statistics | 1804 (out: R2 +4.5 dB) |

Reading: every public diffraction construction either applies the same
obstruction adjustment as 707, or drops it (1803), or replaces the surface
with ITM's effective heights (1804). Both of the last two have been run and
are closed. The P.1812-8 TODO in the phase plan resolves to "same as 707".
Any other surface would need a construction that no public source states, and
CLAUDE.md forbids invented constants and unsourced model parts. The text
extraction of P.1812-8 and P.2001-5 was garbled in places (sums and
primes), so the equation numbers come from a reading of the text, not a
symbolic check; the conclusion rests on the presence of the same
obstruction-lowering step in each, which both texts state.

## What stays open

D20a showed that the smooth surface, not the troposcatter bound, carries the
height-gain flattening (only 1804 restores it), and B23 D23a found 707 gives
too little loss reduction when the fixed antenna is raised on obstructed open
paths (+5.6 dB at R1, +3.1 at R2, +1.7 at R3; precision rule not met). So the
defect is real; the lead is that no sourced alternative exists to test it.

Options, none started:

1. **Hold R1** until a new public source appears (a later P.452, P.1812 or
   P.2001 revision, or an NTIA report on the delta construction).
2. **R4 (line-of-sight A_s)** or **R2 (cascade correction C)** as the B21
   candidate. B20's rule left R2 unrun (C is 6 to 22 % of the loss); R4 has not
   been through a diagnostic.
3. **Prospective measurements** (N10 in the post-B20 plan) that separate the
   height response from clutter and calibration.

I recommend 1 and 2 in parallel: keep R1 on hold, and run one diagnostic on R4
before registering a candidate. This needs Nick's decision.
