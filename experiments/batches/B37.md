## 1. Questions

B36 found, on R1's gentle open rows (siting O, uncensored, 1 GHz or less,
steepest interval 10° or less; 1,427 rows on 29 paths, one receiver site):

- 707 predicts more loss than stock ITM, by about 2.5 dB on average, and
  more than was measured (mean signed error +3.17 dB, stock +0.62).
- The 2D PE reference agreed with that direction, but the fresh review
  found three defects in the reference, each of which pushes it toward too
  little loss (B36 section 7, Reference-validity findings).

B37 asks:

1. **D37a.** With those three defects repaired and validated against
   closed-form references, does the PE still say 707's extra loss on these
   rows goes the wrong way?
2. **D37b.** Which part of 707 adds the extra loss? 707 on siting-O rows is
   stock ITM plus two changes: the delta diffraction construction with a
   cascaded multi-edge loss (`diffraction = "delta_cascaded_isolated"`,
   ITU-R P.452-18 eq. (38)–(40) form with ITU-R P.526-11 section 4.4.2 and
   ITU-R P.526-16 section 2.5), and the troposcatter-line bound
   (`diffraction_bound = "itm_troposcatter_line"`). 707's terminal clutter
   term doesn't act at siting O.
3. **D37c (descriptive only).** With the repaired PE, what does the B36
   rule give on the B36 population?

Prior work this builds on: B20 (D20a, D20b), B23 and B25 recorded 707's
delta parts (L_ba, L_bs, A_s) on every Phase 2 row
(`experiments/D23m-metadata-audit/parent_parts_rev1.csv`) and found a
height-gain inversion on R1-005-T5_O at 230 MHz carried by A_s. D37b reuses
the same reporting (`info.delta_parts`) and adds the step-by-step
attribution on B36's population.

## 2. D37a: PE repairs (no measured value)

A new file, `experiments/batches/pe2d_rev2.m`; `pe2d_rev1.m` stays as run
for B36. Each repair is off by option so rev 2 with all repairs off must
reproduce rev 1 bit for bit on the B36 rows (check A0).

| Repair | Defect (B36) | Method and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-avg | The 20-wavelength average was one point on 1,381 of 1,683 rows | In the final 20λ of range, step at most λ/2 and average power over every step. Window length kept from B36 (planner-chosen, registered there); only the sampling changes. |
| R-imp | Perfect-conductor ground can't give the loss of a real ground | Leontovich impedance boundary for horizontal polarization, by the discrete mixed Fourier transform (Dockery and Kuttler, IEEE Trans. Antennas Propag. 44(12), 1996). Ground constants: the evaluator's `settings_rev1` (ε_r = 25, σ = 0.02 S/m), the values ITM is run with, so the two models see the same ground. |
| R-rough | Smooth 30 m facets reflect coherently | Arm, not default: the specular reflection reduced by the Miller, Brown and Vegh factor (IEE Proc. 131 H(2), 1984) with σ_h from ITM's own `sigma_h_function(terrain_roughness(d, Δh))` (Longley and Rice 1968), applied as an effective impedance at the specular grazing angle of each row. |
| R-wide | Narrow-angle error isn't screened | Run every row with both propagators already in rev 1 ("narrow", Ryan eq. (110); "wide", the exact square-root operator). A path is out of the reference's domain if the two differ by more than 1 dB on any of its rows. |

New validation tests, `pe2d_tests_rev2.m`, written before the code (pass
conditions; the B36 tests T1 to T6 must still pass):

- **T7, impedance flat ground.** 230, 910 MHz; terminals 2 to 20 m; 1 to
  20 km. Reference: 2D two-ray with the Fresnel reflection coefficient for
  horizontal polarization at the same ε_r and σ. Pass: |error| ≤ 0.2 dB at
  every point outside nulls deeper than 20 dB.
- **T8, wide-angle on a 10° plane.** Two-ray of the tilted plane, as T6.
  Pass: "wide" within 0.3 dB; report "narrow"'s error (expected larger).
- **T9, range average.** Flat conducting ground, receiver in the
  interference region. Reference: the analytic average of the two-ray power
  over the same 20λ window. Pass: within 0.1 dB, and the window holds at
  least 40 samples on every B36 row.
- **T10, roughness limit.** With σ_h = 0, R-rough equals R-imp to
  1e-9 dB.
- **A0, parity.** All repairs off reproduces `pe_predictions_rev1.csv`
  A_pe_db exactly (max difference 0).
- **A1, convergence.** The B36 per-path screen (λ/8 vertical step, half
  range step, 1 dB) on every path, for the default repaired arm.

If a test still fails after three passes, D37a stops; D37b runs anyway
(it doesn't use the PE), and D37c doesn't run.

**Arms run on the B36 rows:** rev1 (from B36, not rerun); rev2-default
(R-avg, R-imp, R-wide screen); rev2-rough (plus R-rough). Reported for
each: rows beyond the 6.02 dB two-ray gain limit, rows below free-space
loss, and paths out of domain by R-wide.

## 3. D37b: attribution of 707 − stock on R1's gentle rows (model only)

**Population:** B36's R1 stratum, 1,427 rows on 29 paths, recomputed by
the driver; stop if the count differs. VA's 243 rows are run too and
reported descriptively.

**Arms** (existing `params_exp` switches; each row at 50 %, the
evaluator's settings, the profile of record):

| Arm | Settings | Isolates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | stock | baseline |
| C1 | `diffraction = "delta_bullington"`, bound none | the delta construction with Bullington |
| C2 | `diffraction = "delta_cascaded_isolated"`, bound none | the cascade in place of Bullington |
| C3 | C2 plus `diffraction_bound = "itm_troposcatter_line"` = 707 at siting O | the bound |
| C4 | C3 plus `smooth_reference = "p452_spherical"` | the A_s term (B25's alternative) |

Checks: C3 must equal `var_lb_50` in
`experiments/707r-rescore-screen36/predictions_phase2.csv` to 1e-9 dB on
every row, and S must equal `itm_lb_50`; otherwise stop.

**Recorded per row:** loss for each arm; ITM's mode (line of sight,
diffraction, scatter) and whether the delta applies (transhorizon only,
`diffraction_region = "transhorizon"`); 707's parts L_ba, L_bs, A_s,
A_s − L_bs, bound_binds.

**Statistics** (mean over rows, 95 % interval over paths, 2,000 draws,
seed 20261137, numpy default_rng, paths sorted by `path_id`, draws of shape
(2000, 29)):

- Steps: Δ1 = C1 − S, Δ2 = C2 − C1, Δ3 = C3 − C2. Δ1 + Δ2 + Δ3 = C3 − S
  by construction; this identity is a check, not evidence.
- By ITM mode, and by whether the delta applies.
- On delta rows: mean L_ba − L_bs and mean max(A_s − L_bs, 0), and the
  share of rows where max(A_s − L_bs, 0) > 0.
- C4 − C3: does B25's spherical-earth reference change the R1 excess?

**Reading rule (decided before the run):** a step "carries" the excess if
its mean is at least half of mean(C3 − S) and its interval excludes zero.
If no step does, the result is "spread", and no single switch is a lead.

## 4. D37c: B36's rule with the repaired PE (descriptive only)

Runs only if D37a passes. B36's rule, unchanged (section 4 there: e =
L − meas_lb_db; D = mean |e_PE| − mean |e_707|; R1 then VA; resolved means
|D| ≥ 1 dB and the interval excludes zero), seed 20261136, on the B36 rows
the repaired PE keeps in domain. Also the PE against each D37b arm on R1.

This isn't a test: B36 read these measured values with the same rule, and
the repairs were chosen after seeing B36's result. It can't promote
anything or support a claim that the PE "beats" 707. It answers only
whether B36's R1 direction survives the repairs.
