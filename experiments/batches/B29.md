## 1. Why

B28 couldn't fit 707: the calibrator held each fold's training censored
violations to stock ITM's rate + 1 point, and 707 itself is 3.92 points over
that on fold 4 (R1 and R2 censored rows). Nick, 2026-11-25, approved
rerunning with the constraint referenced to the parent ("Do all 3"). The
acceptance rule in FRAMEWORK.md gates a child's censored violations against
its parent (+1 point), so the new reference matches how every other change
is judged. The change to `eval/cv_calibrate_rev1.m` is an option,
`CensoredRef="base"`; the default stays `"stock"`.

The physical rationale for each constant group is unchanged from B28
section 1.
