## Purpose

707's `itm_troposcatter_line` bound has no published source (registration
rev 2, known defect 3, raised by two of three reviewers). It extends ITM's
scatter line, fitted at d_ML + 200 km and d_ML + 400 km, back to paths just
past the horizon. There it caps the multi-edge loss on 1,345 Phase 2 rows by
a median of 12.9 dB, flattens height gain, and leaves 707 9 to 16 dB
optimistic at R2 above 1.8 GHz.

A model-to-model comparison on development rows suggested the alternative:
take the smaller of the diffraction loss and the troposcatter loss
**computed for the path itself**, at its own distance and angular distance,
with nothing extrapolated. B13 (1301) tested P.452-18 eq. (45) inside ITM's
scatter line, which kept the extrapolation; this batch doesn't.

## Code

- `itm_p2p_core_exp.m`: new `cfg.diffraction_bound = "p452_troposcatter_at_d"`.
  - On every transhorizon path (ITM mode not line of sight) at 100 MHz and
    above: A_ref = min(A_ref, max(L_bs − L_fs, 0)).
  - L_bs is ITU-R P.452-18 section 4.3 eq. (45) at 50 % time
    (`p452_troposcatter_rev1`, B13). It uses the path's own d, and
    θ = θ_t + θ_r + d/a_e (P.452-18 Table 4 definition), from ITM's
    profile horizon angles and effective curvature.
  - Below 100 MHz, outside P.452-18's scope, 707's line bound applies
    unchanged.
  - The deviations from P.452-18 are B13's: ITM's a_e, not the ΔN map, and
    G_t + G_r = 0 dBi (TODO).
  - No new constant.
- `params_exp.m`: documents the option. The default stays `"none"`.
- Test `test_b19_p452_bound_at_d`:
  - hand value at 450, 1,800 and 5,000 MHz on a transhorizon ridge path
  - the bound never raises the loss
  - equals 707's bound at 60 MHz
  - no change in line of sight
- The full suite passes: 48 of 48 on the Mac.
