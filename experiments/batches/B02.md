# Batch B02: Epstein-Peterson without its two failure modes

Planner: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-21. Worker: the session model. The
predictions below were committed before any scored run of these settings.

## What B01 showed

- **101 (Epstein-Peterson) is the only structural change so far that beats
  stock ITM and R02:** composite 15.3 against 16.0 and 15.9, with the paired
  gain clearly above zero on both composites. Two things blocked it.
- **Blocker 1, a jump at ITM's line-of-sight boundary.** Planner diagnosis on
  the battery's ridge sweep: the 20.7 dB step sits exactly at d_sML
  (26.1 km), where ITM's region changes from line of sight (mode 1) to
  diffraction (mode 2). B01 applied the new loss only on the transhorizon
  side.
- **Blocker 2, censored violations 30.0 % (limit 29.0 %).** 37 of the 42 new
  violations are at Virginia, with a median margin of 1.3 dB. Virginia is
  the site where bare-earth models already predict too little loss because
  of clutter (mean error −8.1 dB). The gate stands; this batch doesn't
  target it.
- **A third problem the same sweep exposed: a sampling artifact.** When a
  ridge top falls between two 30 m samples, each terminal's horizon is a
  different sample, and Epstein-Peterson treats one steep edge as two
  near-grazing edges of about 6 dB each. The loss drops about 10 dB, and it
  swings by that much as the distance changes by one sample (177.9, 187.4,
  178.1 dB on the sweep). So part of 101's gain on mountain paths may be
  this artifact.
- More edges (102, 103) added loss and made things worse. Bullington (104)
  kept censored violations under 33 %, so the references' optimism on dead
  paths doesn't come from single-edge diffraction.

## What the new code does

Code in `itm_variant/multi_edge_diffraction_rev1.m` and
`itm_p2p_core_exp.m`; tests in `itm_variant/tests/test_diffraction_rev1.m`
(12 of 12 pass). Parity at the default settings: 6.8 × 10⁻¹³ dB. No new
free parameters; every constant comes from the cited equation.

- `cfg.diffraction = "ep_isolated"`: the two horizon obstacles count as two
  edges only if they're isolated in the sense of ITU-R P.526-16 section 2.5:
  every profile point between them clears the line joining their tops by at
  least 0.6 of the first Fresnel zone radius (P.526-16 eq. (2)). Otherwise
  they're one obstacle sampled twice, and the loss is the single knife edge
  of the highest-ν point.
- `cfg.diffraction = "p526_double_edge"`: ITU-R P.526-16 section 4.3,
  L = L1 + L2 + Lc (eq. (39), (40)), with Lc applied only where the
  Recommendation says it's valid (each of L1 and L2 above about 15 dB).
  Same edges and isolation test as `"ep_isolated"`.
- `cfg.diffraction_region = "blocked_paths"`: the profile-based loss also
  applies to line-of-sight-region paths whose profile blocks the direct ray.
  ITM's line-of-sight fit passes through the diffraction line at d_sML, so
  the loss is continuous there with no taper and no new constant.

Planner code check (physics battery only; no measurements read): both
B02 settings pass the full battery. Each fix alone fails: `"ep_isolated"`
without `"blocked_paths"` fails the ridge sweep (11.5 dB step against
8.0 dB), and Bullington with `"blocked_paths"` fails the rough sweep
(34.3 against 33.0 dB). Those two weren't scheduled.

## Experiments

Reference: stock ITM composite 16.0, screened 13.4, Phase 2 cell 18.3,
long-term level 24.6, shape 5.2, censored 28.0 %; site RMS R1 13.3,
R2 28.6 (mean +10.6), R3 9.0, VA 15.4. 101 for comparison: composite 15.3,
screened 13.0, R2 26.0 (mean +8.9), censored 30.0 %.

### 201-ep-isolated-blocked
- Change: `cfg.diffraction = "ep_isolated";` and
  `cfg.diffraction_region = "blocked_paths";`
- Tier 2. Functions: `longley_rice` diffraction line, both regions;
  `multi_edge_diffraction_rev1`.
- Source: Epstein and Peterson 1953 (NTIA TR-26-580 section 3.3.2); ITU-R
  P.526-16 section 2.5 isolation criterion and eq. (2).
- Rationale: 101's mechanism, with the sampling artifact removed and the
  loss continuous at ITM's line-of-sight boundary.
- Predicted: the artifact removal adds loss back on ridge tops between
  samples, and the extension changes blocked line-of-sight-region paths
  (direction uncertain). Net: less gain than 101. Composite 15.4–15.9; R2
  RMS 26–28 (mean +8 to +10); R1, R3, VA within 1.0 dB of stock; censored
  28.5–31 %. Physics battery: pass. About even odds of clearing every
  gate; the censored gate and the 0.3 dB screened-gain threshold are the
  likely failures.
- Confidence: low on size.

### 202-p526-double-edge-blocked
- Change: `cfg.diffraction = "p526_double_edge";` and
  `cfg.diffraction_region = "blocked_paths";`
- Alternative to 201.
- Source: ITU-R P.526-16 section 4.3, eq. (39), (40); section 2.5.
- Rationale: the Recommendation's own two-edge method adds the separation
  correction Lc for strongly shadowed isolated edge pairs.
- Predicted: same as 201 on most paths; slightly more loss on isolated
  pairs that both exceed 15 dB. Composite 0.0–0.3 dB above 201; censored
  0–1 point below 201. Physics battery: pass.
- Confidence: low.
