## Checks (written before the script)

1. **Reproduction.** The script first reproduces these B23 cells from the pair CSVs, to 0.01 dB, or stops:
   D23a R1 O blocked-blocked +5.64 (29 bases), R2 +3.14, R3 +1.65; D23c T at R1 +3.94 (16), VA +4.61, R3 +1.37, R2 +9.10.
2. **Fresnel stratum.** Both the "low-end clearance >= 0.6" and "both-end clearance >= 0.6" subsets
   are reported with base counts. The low-end R1 figure must match the reviewer's +1.25 (11 bases) within 0.05 dB, or the difference is reported.
3. **Leave-one-fixed-terminal-out and leave-one-site-out.** Every cell reports min and max of the
   equal-base mean over the leave-outs, and a bootstrap over fixed terminals where more than one exists.
4. **Censored bounds.** For D23a, the sign of e on every pair, including pairs with a censored run,
   using the frozen intervals. Count pairs whose sign is determined.
5. **Calibration margin.** The mean and lower bound minus 2 dB (the R1 impedance note), reported as a margin, not a correction.
6. **T per decade** for D23c.
7. **Dispositions** use `disposition()` from `b23_common_rev1.py` unchanged. Any cell that changes
   disposition under a sensitivity is listed. A sensitivity can only weaken a B23 reading in the text, never strengthen it.
8. **Scope.** The commit touches only `experiments/` (the guard passes). A fresh reviewer recomputes
   cells 1 to 3 without reading the script.
