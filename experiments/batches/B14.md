The runs need PR #7 (dN at path midpoints) merged first, and they're

## Purpose

B13 showed that a different troposcatter median doesn't help (1301, −0.30 dB);
the long-term error is spread, not level. ITU-R P.617-6 §4.1 gives the
troposcatter time distribution on both sides of 50 %. Test whether its
spread is what the long-term shape and level scores need.

## Source, read from the published PDF

ITU-R P.617-6 (09/2025), §4.1, eqs. (1)-(7b), read from the PDF pages
(text extraction garbles eqs. 6-7, so the equations were read from the
rendered page). See `itm_variant/p617_troposcatter_rev1.m` for the full set.
Interpretations, recorded before any score:

- "log" in eq. (6) is read as log₁₀, the ITU-R convention.
- "sin θ_t / 1 000" in eq. (7a) is read as sin(θ_t / 1000), with θ_t in mrad.
- k a: ITM's effective radius (P.617-6 allows a more accurate value than
  k = 4/3).
- h_s: ground height under the common volume, at eq. (7a)'s range
  d sin β / sin(θ/1000) from the transmitter. P.617-6 defines h_s only as the
  height of the Earth's surface; where to take it is a design choice.
- h_b = 7.35 km, P.617-6's global mean reference value.
- G_t + G_r = 0 dBi: the records carry no antenna gains.
- dN and N_0: the P.617-6 Table 1 maps (DN50, N050) at the path midpoint
  (PR #7). P.617-6 asks for the common volume; on the 1.5° grid the two differ
  little.

## Code (on `main`, defaults keep stock)

- `itm_variant/p617_troposcatter_rev1.m`: eqs. (1)-(7b).
- `itm_variant/refractivity_lookup_rev1.m`: dN by path_id from
  `data/links/path_refractivity_rev1.csv` (PR #7), SHA-256 checked; the
  long-term mean for synthetic battery paths; error for an unknown path.
- `cfg.time_variability`:
  - `"p617"`: ITM's time deviate Y_T replaced by P.617-6 Y_p at
    p = 100 Q(z_T), on long-term rows in ITM's troposcatter region (mode 3).
    Both models subtract the deviate from the median and make it positive
    below 50 %, so it replaces Y_T directly. Deviates within the inverse-normal
    approximation's error (|z_T| ≤ 4.5e-4, Abramowitz and Stegun 26.2.23, as
    used by `itm_percentiles.m`) are taken as exactly 50 %.
  - `"p617_transhorizon"`: the same on every transhorizon long-term path
    (modes 2 and 3).
- `cfg.troposcatter = "p617"`: P.617-6's median (Y_p = 0) as ITM's scatter
  attenuation on long-term rows, as 1301 did with P.452.
- Phase 2 rows are never changed: P.617-6 predicts annual distributions, and
  the Phase 2 records are short-term measurements.

## Physics finding (blocks keep eligibility)

Applying the battery's distance-continuity rule (largest step no more than
1 dB above stock's) at 1 % and 99 % on a rough synthetic profile:

| Setting | Largest step at 1 % | at 99 % | Stock | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `p617` (mode 3 only), 60-400 km | 22.2 dB | 19.7 dB | 17.2, 16.5 | fails by 4-5 dB at ITM's changeover to troposcatter (70 km) |
| `p617_transhorizon`, 10-400 km | 24.3 dB | 26.2 dB | 24.7, 24.7 | fails by 1.5 dB at 99 %, at the radio horizon |

The tails switch abruptly from ITM's deviate to P.617-6's wherever the switch
starts to apply. That's a physical discontinuity, even though the frozen
battery passes. So these runs are registered as **diagnostic D06**: they tell
us whether P.617-6's spread is the right size, but neither can be kept.

Proposed follow-up if D06 shows the shape score improves: a combination that's
continuous by construction, such as the ITU-R P.2001-6 §5 power-sum of the
diffraction and troposcatter sub-models at each time percentage. It would be a
new batch.

Proposed evaluator change (for Nick): add the continuity sweeps at 1 % and
99 % to `physics_battery_rev1.m`, report-only at first, as was done for the
real-path height gain (PR #4). This batch found the gap.
