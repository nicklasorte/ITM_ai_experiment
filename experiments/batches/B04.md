# Batch B04: delta-Bullington reference attenuation

Planner: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-21. Worker: the session model.
Predictions committed before any scored run.

## Why

B03 found that the diffraction swaps of B01–B03 touch only about 10 % of
R2's measured rows; most of R2's +10.6 dB overprediction sits in paths ITM
puts in its line-of-sight region although their profile is blocked. B02
reached those paths by extrapolating ITM's smooth-earth line to them, which
lowered loss by about 10 dB on half of all rows and doubled censored
violations. B04 reaches them by a published construction instead: the
delta-Bullington method (hypothesis H4), which takes the obstruction from
the actual profile and the smooth-earth physics from a smooth-earth
equivalent path.

## Construction

`cfg.diffraction = "delta_bullington"` (or `"delta_bullington_p526"`)
replaces ITM's reference attenuation on every profile path with

- L_ba: Bullington loss of the actual profile with the actual antennas
  (ITU-R P.526-16 section 4.5.1, eq. (49)–(57));
- L_bs: Bullington loss of a zero-height profile with the antennas at their
  heights above the smooth-earth surface;
- A_s: ITM's own reference attenuation for the smooth-earth equivalent path,
  standing in for the spherical-earth loss L_sph.

The smooth-earth surface is the least-squares fit with the obstruction
adjustment and terminal-ground limits of ITU-R P.452-18 Attachment 2
section 5, eq. (161)–(167) (the same construction as ITU-R P.526-16
section 4.5.2), transcribed from and checked against the P.452 port in
`nicklasorte/matlab_itu_p452`. Forms:

| Switch | Formula | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `"delta_bullington"` | L_ba + max(A_s − L_bs, 0) | ITU-R P.452-18 eq. (38)–(40) |
| `"delta_bullington_p526"` | max(L_ba + A_s − L_bs, 0) | ITU-R P.526-16 eq. (66) |

ITM's variability, effective heights, and terrain irregularity Δh for the
statistics are kept from the actual path. No new free parameters.

Checks (planner): 15 of 15 tests pass, including the P.526 form returning
stock ITM's A_ref exactly on straight terrain at three slopes, reciprocity,
and obstacle gain on a single ridge. Both forms pass the full physics
battery (code check; no measurements read). Parity at defaults:
6.8 × 10⁻¹³ dB.

## Experiments

Reference: stock ITM composite 16.0, screened 13.4, Phase 2 cell 18.3,
long-term level 24.6, shape 5.2, censored 28.0 %; R1 13.3, R2 28.6 (mean
+10.6), R3 9.0, VA 15.4 (mean −8.1).

### 401-delta-bullington-p452
- Change: `cfg.diffraction = "delta_bullington";`
- Tier 2. Function: `itm_p2p_core_exp` reference attenuation
  (`delta_bullington_reference`).
- Source: ITU-R P.452-18 eq. (38)–(40), (161)–(167); ITU-R P.526-16
  section 4.5.1.
- Rationale: blocked paths get their obstruction loss from the actual
  profile, and smooth-earth loss only as an excess over the smooth-profile
  Bullington loss, so ITM's extrapolated line no longer sets their loss.
- Predicted: a large change on many rows, so wide ranges. R2 mean error
  falls to between −2 and +6 dB, R2 RMS 20–26; R1 and R3 move by up to
  2 dB; VA gets worse by 0–2 dB (more optimistic at a site already
  optimistic). Long-term level 19–24. Composite 13.5–15.5. Censored
  violations 30–45 %: the most likely reason for rejection.
- Confidence: low.

### 402-delta-bullington-p526
- Change: `cfg.diffraction = "delta_bullington_p526";`
- Alternative to 401.
- Source: ITU-R P.526-16 section 4.5.2, eq. (66).
- Rationale: the Recommendation's own form, which lets a negative
  smooth-earth excess lower the obstruction loss.
- Predicted: loss at or below 401 on every path (tested), so a slightly
  lower composite or similar, and censored 1–5 points above 401.
- Confidence: low.

## Order

401, then 402. Alternatives; at most one becomes `best`.
