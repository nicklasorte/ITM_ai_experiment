## 2. Source

Longley, A. G. and Hufford, G. A., *Sensor Path Loss Measurements: Analysis
and Comparison with Propagation Models*, OT Report 75-74, Office of
Telecommunications, U.S. Department of Commerce, October 1975. Public (ITS,
`https://its.ntia.gov/publications/download/OT-75-74.pdf`; SHA-256
079d0cc6ce4165f9f019d0dfa739e98344d095aa38beeb389c7cc262b6c38a5e). Not a FRAMEWORK.md Tier B set.

Contents used:

- Tables 7 to 11: 10, 50 and 90 % basic transmission loss for every path at
  VHF and UHF (172 and 410 MHz per the report) with receiving antennas at
  10, 3 and 0.3 m. Table 7: Eglin AFB, Florida (flat, heavy forest). Tables
  8 and 9: Graham Mountain area, Arizona (September and October 1973; April
  1974). Tables 10 and 11: Hunter Liggett area, California (January and
  February 1974; May and June 1974). Values marked * are questionable in
  the source; ">" values are lower bounds.
- Figures 10 and 16 (and the Hunter Liggett map): site maps with latitude
  and longitude ticks and a scale bar.

## 3. Steps and checks

1. **Digitize Tables 7 to 11 twice, independently:** (a) the scan's text
   layer, cleaned by script (`d31_parse_text_rev1.py`); (b) a visual
   transcription of the page images by a separate agent that doesn't see
   (a). A script compares every cell. Check: every disagreement is resolved
   by reading the page image and logged; the final table records the
   source's * and > flags.
2. **Locate the terminals** from the site maps, by linear georeferencing on
   the printed graticule ticks. Check: the great-circle distance between
   each transmitter and receiver is within 5 % or 0.25 km (whichever is
   larger) of the tabulated distance for at least 90 % of paths; failures
   are listed.
3. **Model comparison (later, on the Mac):** a separately registered rule,
   written before any model run, comparing measured and predicted height
   contrasts (10 m minus 3 m, 3 m minus 0.3 m) for 707 and stock ITM, by
   area and frequency.
