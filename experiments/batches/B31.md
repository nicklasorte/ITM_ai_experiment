## 2. Source

Longley, A. G. and Hufford, G. A., *Sensor Path Loss Measurements: Analysis
and Comparison with Propagation Models*, OT Report 75-74, Office of
Telecommunications, U.S. Department of Commerce, October 1975. Public (ITS,
`https://its.ntia.gov/publications/download/OT-75-74.pdf`; SHA-256
079d0cc6ce4165f9f019d0dfa739e98344d095aa38beeb389c7cc262b6c38a5e). Not a FRAMEWORK.md Tier B set.

Contents used:

- Tables 7 to 11: 10, 50 and 90 % basic transmission loss for every path at
  VHF and UHF (Appendix A: transmitters tuned to 172.5 and 410.5 MHz;
  section 2 calls them 172 and 410 MHz) with receiving antennas at 10, 3
  and 0.35 m (the tables label the lowest 0.3 m). Table 7: Eglin AFB, Florida (flat, heavy forest). Tables
  8 and 9: Graham Mountain area, Arizona (September and October 1973; April
  1974). Tables 10 and 11: Hunter Liggett area, California (January and
  February 1974; May and June 1974). Values marked * are questionable in
  the source; ">" values are lower bounds.
- Figures 10 and 16 (and the Hunter Liggett map): site maps with latitude
  and longitude ticks and a scale bar.

## 3. Steps and checks

1. **Digitize Tables 7 to 11 twice, independently:** (a) the scan's text
   layer, cleaned by script (`d31_parse_text_rev1.py`); (b) a visual
   transcription of the page images by a separate agent that doesn't see
   (a). A script compares every cell. Check: every disagreement is resolved
   by reading the page image and logged; the final table records the
   source's * and > flags.
2. **Locate the terminals.** Revised 2026-11-26, before any positions were
   read: (a) read coarse positions of every terminal from the site maps,
   georeferenced on the printed graticule ticks and the scale bar (the
   Hunter Liggett map prints one meridian and one parallel, so the scale bar
   sets distance); (b) refine all positions in each area by least squares against
   the tabulated transmitter-receiver distances (every T-R pair is
   measured, 40 or more distances per area), with the map positions as a
   weak prior (planner-chosen weight: 100 m standard deviation); the area's
   overall placement comes from the map. Checks: the refined distances
   match the tabulated ones with RMS under 0.03 km; every refined position
   is within 300 m of its map position; failures are listed. The map
   positions are the independent check on the refinement.
   Note from the report (section 2): in all three areas the transmitting
   antennas had their center of radiation 0.35 m above ground, at nominal
   172 and 410 MHz, and the receiving antennas were at 10, 3 and 0.35 m
   (the tables label the lowest 0.3 m). Only the receiving height varies.
3. **Model comparison (later, on the Mac):** a separately registered rule,
   written before any model run, comparing measured and predicted height
   contrasts (10 m minus 3 m, 3 m minus 0.3 m) for 707 and stock ITM, by
   area and frequency.
