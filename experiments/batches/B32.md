## 1. Question

On paths the search has never seen, does 707 predict the change in basic
transmission loss with receiving antenna height better than stock ITM does?

## 3. Registered rule

For each path, band and table, the measured contrasts are
C_hi = L(3 m) − L(10 m) and C_lo = L(0.35 m) − L(3 m), at 50 %. (B31
step 3 wrote "10 m minus 3 m"; this sign is used instead, so a positive
contrast is extra loss at the lower antenna.) The same contrasts come from
each model. The contrast error is e = C_model − C_measured.

Strata: table (7 to 11) × band (VHF, UHF) × contrast (hi, lo); 20 strata.
In each stratum: mean |e| for 707 and for stock ITM, and the paired
difference D = mean |e_707| − mean |e_stock|, with a 95 % percentile
interval from a bootstrap over paths (2,000 draws, seed 1). Strata with
fewer than 10 paths are descriptive. The reviewer's count of paths per
stratum after the exclusions (R5 removed) is 16 to 39, so all 20 strata
count:

| Table | VHF hi | VHF lo | UHF hi | UHF lo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | 39 | 35 | 37 | 37 |
| 8 | 35 | 29 | 18 | 16 |
| 9 | 33 | 37 | 39 | 39 |
| 10 | 31 | 33 | 31 | 18 |
| 11 | 34 | 30 | 28 | 22 |

The script recomputes these counts and stops if they differ.

Outcomes:

- **A (707 better):** D < 0 with its interval excluding zero in at least 12
  of the 20 strata, and D > 0 with its interval excluding zero in none.
- **B (stock better):** the mirror image.
- **C (no clear difference):** anything else.

Scope: the exclusions drop mostly lower-bound cells at 0.35 m and UHF, so
the largest C_lo values are missing; every claim is limited to paths with
both heights measured.

Also reported, not deciding: mean signed e by stratum; each model's
absolute 50 % error at each height; and, per stratum, the number of paths
where 707 and stock ITM differ by less than 0.01 dB at both heights,
labelled "identical by construction" (for example line-of-sight paths
where 707's diffraction changes don't act).

All thresholds (12 strata, 10 paths) and the Eglin conductivity are
planner-chosen.

### 3a. Amendment, 2026-11-26, before any model output (Nick's choice)

The section 5 smoke check stopped at the first model call: ITM v1.4
rejects terminal heights below 0.5 m (NTIA `ValidateInputs.cpp`, error
1000 for the transmitter and 1001 for the receiver; `itm_p2p_rev1` and
`itm_p2p_core_exp` both raise it, with or without `validity="off"`). Every
transmitter here is at 0.35 m, and so are the lowest receivers. No loss
had been computed when this was found.

- **Model heights:** both models use 0.5 m, ITM's lower limit, wherever
  the report gives 0.35 m. Measured values are unchanged.
- **Deciding contrast:** C_hi only (10 strata: table × band). Outcome A
  needs D < 0 with its interval excluding zero in at least 6 of the 10, and
  D > 0 with its interval excluding zero in none; B is the mirror; C
  otherwise. 6 of 10 keeps the original 12-of-20 share.
- **C_lo:** reported as descriptive only, because the model contrast is
  L(0.5 m) − L(3 m) against a measured L(0.35 m) − L(3 m).
- **Scope:** the transmitter offset (0.5 m modeled, 0.35 m measured) is
  common to both heights of each contrast and to both models, so it mostly
  cancels in C_hi; it doesn't cancel in the absolute errors, which stay
  descriptive.

What the results show, scoped to each population:

- **C_hi, deciding:** on these paths neither model predicts the 3 m to
  10 m height contrast better. Mean absolute errors are 4.1 to 5.5 dB in
  nine strata and 2.7 to 2.8 dB in Table 8 UHF.
- **C_hi, descriptive:** in all 10 C_hi strata, 707's mean signed error is
  below stock ITM's: 707 predicts a smaller 3 m to 10 m contrast than stock
  ITM does (by 0.7 to 2.7 dB in the stratum means). That doesn't make it
  closer to the measurements: stock ITM's mean signed error is already
  negative in six of the ten strata.
- **C_lo, descriptive only (section 3a):** both models under-predict C_lo
  in all 10 strata (mean signed e from −2.6 to −10.7 dB). Part of this is
  by construction: the models' lower antenna is at 0.5 m, not 0.35 m, which
  lowers modeled loss at the lower antenna and so lowers the modeled C_lo.
  This run doesn't size that part.
- **Absolute 50 % error, descriptive:** the transmitter offset (0.5 m
  modeled, 0.35 m measured) doesn't cancel here, and the 0.35 m receiver
  cells also carry the receiver clamp, so these numbers carry both offsets
  for both models. Both models predict less loss than measured at almost
  every table, band and height; Eglin (Table 7) is furthest off, with mean
  signed errors of −13.7 to −27.4 dB for 707 and −19.3 to −29.7 dB for stock
  ITM. At VHF, 707's mean absolute error is lower than stock ITM's at every
  table and height, by 2.5 to 5.5 dB, because 707 adds loss where both
  under-predict; at UHF neither is consistently lower. These cells are
  reported, not deciding (section 3).
- **Where 707 acts:** 707's propagation mode is diffraction-dominated on
  1,124 of 1,260 rows (852 double-horizon, 272 single-horizon) and line of
  sight on 136. 707 and stock ITM differ by less than 0.01 dB on 2 rows, so
  no stratum has a path that's identical by construction.

Predictions against outcome:

- Outcome C (55 %): matched.
- Table 7 C_lo, both models under-predict in both bands: true (VHF −2.80
  and −2.62 dB; UHF −3.00 and −3.08 dB), but the 0.5 m clamp pushes the
  modeled C_lo the same way, so this match doesn't test the forest
  reasoning behind it.

### 6b. Follow-ups, 2026-11-26 (descriptive, after the decision)

Run after section 6 was written; they change nothing in the outcome.
Scripts: `d32_clamp_sensitivity_rev1.m`, `d32_extras_rev1.py`. Outputs:
`clamp_sensitivity_rev1.csv`, `extras_rev1.json`.

- **Size of the 0.5 m receiver clamp:** on rows whose 0.35 m cell passes
  the exclusions (Graham R5 removed; 173 VHF and 141 UHF rows), each model
  was rerun with the receiver at 0.7 and 1.0 m (the 0.5 m rerun matched
  section 6 to 3e-7 dB). Moving the receiver from 0.7 m down to 0.5 m adds
  a median 0.16 dB (707) and 0.10 dB (stock ITM) at VHF, and 0.24 dB and
  0.09 dB at UHF; the 90th percentiles are 0.34 to 0.52 dB. Extrapolated to
  0.35 m (linear in height, or linear in log height, both assumptions, not
  model runs), each model's C_lo would rise by a median of 0.07 to 0.26 dB.
  Against C_lo mean signed errors of −2.6 to −10.7 dB, the clamp explains
  little of the under-prediction in either model, under those
  extrapolations. This bounds only the receiver clamp inside each model's
  own height dependence; the transmitter offset is common to both heights
  of a contrast.
- **Table 7 prediction, revisited:** with the receiver clamp worth about
  0.1 to 0.3 dB, the under-prediction of Eglin's C_lo (about −2.6 to
  −3.1 dB in both bands) isn't mainly the clamp. The prediction's match
  stands as descriptive; the strata are still not deciding.
- **Graham R5 (Table 8, receiver 5; 2 to 9 paths per stratum, so no
  intervals):** C_hi, mean absolute error 3.71 dB (707) and 3.32 dB (stock)
  at VHF on 9 paths, 2.45 and 4.01 dB at UHF on 6; C_lo, 5.81 and 2.87 dB
  at VHF on 7 paths, 5.56 and 2.68 dB at UHF on 2. Too few paths to read a
  direction.
