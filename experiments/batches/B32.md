# Batch B32: height contrasts on the OT 75-74 sensor paths, 707 vs stock ITM (D32, diagnostic, no score)

Planner: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-11-26. Follows B31 (data preparation).

**Status: registered 2026-11-26, not run. Needs the Mac (MATLAB, terrain).**

D-series: nothing here is scored, counts toward k or goes into `data/`.
This data was never used to choose or tune 707, so it's an outside test of
one question, not a validation score.

## 1. Question

On paths the search has never seen, does 707 predict the change in basic
transmission loss with receiving antenna height better than stock ITM does?

## 2. Data and model runs

- Measurements: `experiments/D31-ot7574/ot7574_tables_rev1.csv`, 50 % values
  only. Cells that are missing, lower bounds, questionable or uncertain are
  excluded.
- Geometry: `terminals_all_rev1.csv`. Tables 8 and 9 (Graham, R1 to R4) and
  Tables 10 and 11 (Hunter Liggett) are separate seasons on the same paths;
  each table is its own stratum. Graham R5 (Table 8) is reported but doesn't
  count (10 paths on one receiver).
- Terrain: profiles from transmitter to receiver at 30 m spacing, built
  with the repo's long-term-path method (USGS 3DEP 1 arc-second; the Phase 2
  surveyed-profile method doesn't apply). Builder:
  `experiments/batches/d32_build_profiles_rev1.m`, written and smoke-tested
  before the run.
- Model settings, the same for both models: f = 172 or 410 MHz; transmitter
  0.35 m, receiver 10, 3 or 0.35 m; climate and N_0 from the repo's
  refractivity lookup at the path midpoint; ground constants as the report
  gives per area where stated (Graham: sigma = 10 mS/m, relative
  permittivity 5, report section 6.1), otherwise the repo's defaults;
  vertical polarization (the report's sensor antennas; TODO confirm from the
  report before the run); 50 % time, location and confidence.
- 707: `itm_variant` with 707's switches (`ITMEXP_OVERRIDES` as in
  `b28_calibrate_rev1.m`). Terminal clutter: the report doesn't give the
  Phase 2 siting code, so the run uses siting O at both ends (no terminal
  clutter); this is part of what's tested.
- Stock ITM: `itm_variant` defaults (the `itm` columns).

## 3. Registered rule

For each path, band and table, the measured contrasts are
C_hi = L(3 m) − L(10 m) and C_lo = L(0.35 m) − L(3 m), at 50 %. The same
contrasts are computed from each model. The contrast error is
e = C_model − C_measured.

Strata: table (7 to 11) × band (VHF, UHF) × contrast (hi, lo); 20 strata.
In each stratum: mean |e| for 707 and for stock ITM, and the paired
difference D = mean |e_707| − mean |e_stock|, with a 95 % interval from a
bootstrap over paths (2,000 draws, seed 1). Strata with fewer than 10 paths
are descriptive.

Outcomes:

- **A (707 better):** D < 0 with its interval excluding zero in at least 12
  of the counted strata, and D > 0 with its interval excluding zero in none.
- **B (stock better):** the mirror image.
- **C (no clear difference):** anything else.

Also reported, not deciding: mean signed e by stratum (does either model
over- or under-predict height gain?), and the absolute 50 % error of each
model at each height.

All thresholds (12 strata, 10 paths) are planner-chosen.

## 4. Predictions (planner, before the run)

- Outcome C: 55 %. A: 30 %. B: 15 %.
- Both models under-predict the loss increase from 3 m to 0.35 m (C_lo) at
  Eglin, where heavy forest surrounds low antennas.

1. Smoke: the profile builder and both models run on one path per area.
2. Every profile's end-to-end distance is within 2 % of the tabulated
   distance.
3. The analysis script reproduces a known outcome on synthetic data.
