## 1. Question

On paths the search has never seen, does 707 predict the change in basic
transmission loss with receiving antenna height better than stock ITM does?

## 3. Registered rule

For each path, band and table, the measured contrasts are
C_hi = L(3 m) − L(10 m) and C_lo = L(0.35 m) − L(3 m), at 50 %. (B31
step 3 wrote "10 m minus 3 m"; this sign is used instead, so a positive
contrast is extra loss at the lower antenna.) The same contrasts come from
each model. The contrast error is e = C_model − C_measured.

Strata: table (7 to 11) × band (VHF, UHF) × contrast (hi, lo); 20 strata.
In each stratum: mean |e| for 707 and for stock ITM, and the paired
difference D = mean |e_707| − mean |e_stock|, with a 95 % percentile
interval from a bootstrap over paths (2,000 draws, seed 1). Strata with
fewer than 10 paths are descriptive. The reviewer's count of paths per
stratum after the exclusions (R5 removed) is 16 to 39, so all 20 strata
count:

| Table | VHF hi | VHF lo | UHF hi | UHF lo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | 39 | 35 | 37 | 37 |
| 8 | 35 | 29 | 18 | 16 |
| 9 | 33 | 37 | 39 | 39 |
| 10 | 31 | 33 | 31 | 18 |
| 11 | 34 | 30 | 28 | 22 |

The script recomputes these counts and stops if they differ.

Outcomes:

- **A (707 better):** D < 0 with its interval excluding zero in at least 12
  of the 20 strata, and D > 0 with its interval excluding zero in none.
- **B (stock better):** the mirror image.
- **C (no clear difference):** anything else.

Scope: the exclusions drop mostly lower-bound cells at 0.35 m and UHF, so
the largest C_lo values are missing; every claim is limited to paths with
both heights measured.

Also reported, not deciding: mean signed e by stratum; each model's
absolute 50 % error at each height; and, per stratum, the number of paths
where 707 and stock ITM differ by less than 0.01 dB at both heights,
labelled "identical by construction" (for example line-of-sight paths
where 707's diffraction changes don't act).

All thresholds (12 strata, 10 paths) and the Eglin conductivity are
planner-chosen.

### 3a. Amendment, 2026-11-26, before any model output (Nick's choice)

The section 5 smoke check stopped at the first model call: ITM v1.4
rejects terminal heights below 0.5 m (NTIA `ValidateInputs.cpp`, error
1000 for the transmitter and 1001 for the receiver; `itm_p2p_rev1` and
`itm_p2p_core_exp` both raise it, with or without `validity="off"`). Every
transmitter here is at 0.35 m, and so are the lowest receivers. No loss
had been computed when this was found.

- **Model heights:** both models use 0.5 m, ITM's lower limit, wherever
  the report gives 0.35 m. Measured values are unchanged.
- **Deciding contrast:** C_hi only (10 strata: table × band). Outcome A
  needs D < 0 with its interval excluding zero in at least 6 of the 10, and
  D > 0 with its interval excluding zero in none; B is the mirror; C
  otherwise. 6 of 10 keeps the original 12-of-20 share.
- **C_lo:** reported as descriptive only, because the model contrast is
  L(0.5 m) − L(3 m) against a measured L(0.35 m) − L(3 m).
- **Scope:** the transmitter offset (0.5 m modeled, 0.35 m measured) is
  common to both heights of each contrast and to both models, so it mostly
  cancels in C_hi; it doesn't cancel in the absolute errors, which stay
  descriptive.
